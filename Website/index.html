The Arista Project

AI-powered recommendations for safer, greener crop protection.

The Arista Project is a bio-agtech platform helping farmers transition from synthetic pesticides to bio-based alternatives - using AI, regulatory alignment, and real-world savings.

Why Arista?

The name Arista comes from the Latin for the awn — the fine bristle of wheat. A subtle, protective botanical structure that supports the seed’s growth and dispersal. It symbolises agriculture at its purest: quiet, resilient, and full of promise.
